## Left Side - Startup Flow

16 green and she'll run like a dream!

That's the phrase to remember when using your KC-208 for SafeStart™ assistance.
Each of the 16 handcrafted, two-phase annunciator lamps will tell you one of two things:

Lit - Good state for a safe start.
Unlit - Improper state for a safe start.

Note: 16 green does not indicate a complete checklist for a safe flight.  That responsibility will always fall to you, the PIC.  16 green indicates, and is limited to, necessary conditions for a safe engine start.

## Advisories and Alerts

Our state-of-the-art Advisories panel, located at the top center of your panel, will provide real-time updates on engine status, powerplant exceedance warnings, and critical messages.

Any critical exceedance of an engine or powerplant tolerance will produce an alert message on the Critical Advisory Log screen, located at the far right of your panel. This screen tracks any critical states for the duration of your flight. These are events that will likely lead to engine or powerplant damage if not corrected in a timely manner.

You can remove the last logged event by clicking the `CLR LAST` button on your console.

See the Alerts page for more details.

## Main Stack

In the center of your suite, you will find the heart of this avionics system. Here you will find real-time displays of your PT6A-114A as it carries you through the skies, as well as a bottom stack of convenient navigational data so you don't have to focus on those fussy needles.
Your four primary displays located in the top row also come with an indicator lamp.  Should you exceed a maximum operating parameter, the corresponding lamp will illuminate.

# Kabocha Cruise™ {#cruise}

Just below your top rack, you will notice four small liquid-crystal displays. This is where our proprietary Kabocha Cruise™ system comes into play.

When you've reached your cruising altitude, Kabocha Cruise™ activates and uses a bleeding-edge mathematical concept called Kabochapolation™. With this technology, Kabocha Cruise™ will calculate the ideal power settings for a fuel-efficient, safe, and reliable cruise. Guaranteed to save you money on costly repair bills, fuel, and probably other things.

Note: During T/O and climb phases, these screens will provide your maximum operating values, not suggested values.  Use your best judgement when operating your aircraft.

Disclaimer: Kabocha Cruise™ basically infers the optimal settings for normal cruise at a given pressure altitude based on the POH. These are practical reference targets, not mandatory power settings. > Use the small reference LCDs on the four primary cards for the active phase values.

# Alerts {#alerts}

## Starter and Hung Start

Starter timing uses actual starter-motor activity rather than switch position. A warning appears at 35 seconds, and ABORT appears at 40 seconds.

## Hot Start

HOT START applies when the turbine is actively turning below 12 percent Ng and the condition lever is moved out of CUTOFF. A cold engine at zero Ng does not trigger it. Hot starts can produce catastrophic engine damage in a matter of seconds. If this warning appears, move the condition lever to CUTOFF immediately and reduce throttle.

## Propeller Overspeed

Above 1900 RPM for more than two seconds produces a live alert. More than 15 seconds logs a limit event. Above 2090 RPM for more than two seconds logs an event immediately.

## Fuel Imbalance

At a 200 lb difference, the display advises selecting OFF the lower-fuel tank. At a 250 lb difference, a critical event is logged.

## Oil Pressure

Post-ignition oil-pressure monitoring looks for a substantial, unexpected drop while suppressing the expected pressure decay during normal shutdown.
